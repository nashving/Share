## Introduction to Navadvipa-mandala

Navadvipa-mandala is a sixteen-krosa (approximately 32-mile) sacred territory in the Nadia district of Bengal, India, consisting of nine islands (*dvipas*) formed by the branches of the River Ganga. Together they form the shape of an eight-petalled lotus, with Antardvipa at the center as the whorl. Each island corresponds to one of the nine limbs of devotional service (*nava-vidha bhakti*), and each corresponds to a forest (*vana*) of Vraja-mandala.

The nine islands in parikrama sequence:

| Island | Bhakti-anga | Vraja Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Antardvipa | Atma-nivedanam (full surrender) | Gokula Mahavana |
| Simantadvipa | Sravanam (hearing) | Mathura / Madhuvana |
| Godrumadvipa | Kirtanam (chanting) | Nandagrama / Nandisvarapura |
| Madhyadvipa | Smaranam (remembering) | — |
| Koladvipa | Pada-sevanam (serving the lotus feet) | Bahulavana / Dvaraka |
| Rtudvipa | Arcanam (worship) | Radha-kunda / Syama-kunda |
| Jahnudvipa | Vandanam (praying) | Bhadravana |
| Modadrumadvipa | Dasyam (becoming a servant) | Bhandira-vana / Ayodhya |
| Rudradvipa | Sakhyam (being a friend) | Vrindavana / Pulina |

The parikrama begins at Yoga-pitha in Mayapur (Antardvipa), proceeds south and west across the Ganga, circles through all eight outer islands, and returns to Mayapur. Lord Caitanya Himself would perform this parikrama, and it was mapped and described by Lord Nityanananda Prabhu to Sri Jiva Gosvami, as recorded in the *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* of Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura.

## Sequence of Parikrama

Lord Nityanananda describes the parikrama sequence: "Start sankirtana from the Lord's house and go towards Barakona-ghata. Then proceed towards Sridhara's house and come to the end of Antardvipa. After that, cross the Ganga, visiting each island in turn, finally crossing back and returning to Mayapur."

The *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* Pramana-khanda (4.32–4.54) gives a condensed summary of the Lord's own parikrama:

> Antardvipa → Simantadvipa → Godrumadvipa → Madhyadvipa → Brahmana Puskara → Uccahatta → Panca-veni → *cross Ganga* → Koladvipa → Samudragarh → Campahatti → Rtudvipa (Radha-kunda) → Vidyanagara → Jahnudvipa → Modadrumadvipa → Vaikunthapura → Rudradvipa → Rasa-mandala → return to Mayapur.

## 1. Antardvipa — Atma-nivedanam (Full Surrender)

**Vraja equivalent:** Gokula Mahavana  
**Corresponding to:** The center of the eight-petalled lotus; Brahmapura of the Chandogya Upanisad

Antardvipa is the central and first island of parikrama — the birthplace of Lord Sri Gauranga Mahaprabhu. The circumference of this island is ten miles, situated on the eastern bank of the Ganga.

### Places to Visit

**Yoga-pitha (Birthplace of Lord Gauranga)**  
The most sacred spot in all of Navadvipa-mandala — where Lord Sri Krsna Caitanya Mahaprabhu appeared on the full moon night of the month of Phalguna (approximately 1486 CE), during a lunar eclipse, as the son of Jagannatha Misra and Saci-devi. The birthplace (*yoga-pitha*) in Mayapur is identified as the same as the *antara-akasa* described in the Chandogya Upanisad's description of Brahmapura.

Lord Caitanya's eternal pastimes here are described in *Navadvipa-bhava-taranga*: the Lord is served by His eternal parents and associates, by His two wives Laksmipriya and Visnupriya, and manifests in the wondrous five-fold form of Panca-tattva.

**Srivasa Angana**  
The courtyard of Srivasa Pandita where Lord Caitanya performed His famous *antara-kirtana* (private nighttime kirtanas) for one year with His intimate associates. Many extraordinary pastimes took place here, including revelations of the Lord's divine nature to various devotees.

**Sri Advaita Bhavan**  
The residence of Sri Advaita Acarya, who is considered an incarnation of Maha-Visnu and Sadashiva combined. Advaita Acarya's intense worship of Lord Krsna with Ganges water and tulasi — crying out for the Lord's descent — is said to have caused the Lord to appear.

**Sri Gadadhara Angana**  
The residence of Gadadhara Pandita, who is an incarnation of Srimati Radharani in the mood of Lalita-sakhi. Gadadhara Pandita's intimate service to the Lord and his deep spiritual realizations are associated with this place.

**Sri Caitanya Matha**  
Established by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura. The samadhi of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta is here.

**Bharadvaja-tila**  
The hermitage hillock of the sage Bharadvaja Rsi, who performed austerities here and received blessings related to the appearance of Lord Gauranga.

**Murari Gupta Sripata**  
The residence of Murari Gupta, an intimate associate of the Lord who was an incarnation of Hanuman. His unwavering surrender to Lord Ramacandra — and his simultaneous service to Lord Gauranga — are the pastimes of this place.

**Sridhara Angana**  
The residence of Sridhara, the banana-seller devotee, where Lord Caitanya would go to quarrel playfully over the price of bananas and banana flowers. The Lord would reveal His divine nature to Sridhara in moments of transcendental vision.

**Nandana Acarya's House**  
Where Lord Nityananda first stayed when He arrived in Navadvipa, and where the famous first meeting of Lord Gauranga and Lord Nityananda took place. Lord Gauranga, through the agency of Srivasa Pandita reciting *Srimad-Bhagavatam* (SB 10.21.5), caused Lord Nityananda to display extraordinary ecstatic symptoms, including rolling on the ground, roaring, jumping high in the air, and finally fainting. Lord Gauranga then took Nityananda on His lap, comparing the scene to Ramacandra holding the wounded Laksmana.

**Why the name "Antardvipa":**  
In the Brahma-vimohana pastime (Dvapara-yuga), Brahma had stolen Krsna's calves and cowherd boys to test whether Krsna was God. After being defeated, humbled, and forgiven, Brahma came to this place (then called Atopur) and worshiped Lord Gauranga. The Lord appeared and granted Brahma's prayer to take birth as Haridasa Thakura. The name "Antardvipa" reflects the *antara* (inner/hidden) quality of this supreme place, the *antara-akasa* of the Chandogya Upanisad.

## 2. Simantadvipa — Sravanam (Hearing)

**Vraja equivalent:** Mathura city; Madhuvana forest  
**Location:** South of the Ganga, Simuliya-grama area

Simantadvipa is the first island outside the center, visited at the beginning of parikrama by proceeding south from Antardvipa along the Bhaktisiddhanta Road.

### Places to Visit

**Simantini-mandira**  
The temple of Parvati-devi in her form as Simantini. Goddess Parvati came here in Satya-yuga after Lord Siva revealed to her the secret of Lord Gauranga's coming appearance. She performed intense worship here and received darshana of the Lord. She took the dust of His lotus feet and placed it on her *simanta* (the part of her hair), from which the island takes its name. Parvati remains here eternally as Simantini-devi (in one form) and as Paudha Maya-devi in Mayapur (in another form), for the pleasure of Lord Gauranga.

**Chand Kazi's Samadhi**  
The Chand Kazi was the Muslim magistrate (*kazi*) who, acting under the orders of the Muslim king Hussein Shah, broke a mridanga drum and threatened to stop Lord Caitanya's sankirtana movement. Lord Caitanya led a massive nagara-sankirtana procession of hundreds of thousands of devotees carrying torches to confront the Kazi. When the Lord appeared before the Kazi in the form of Nrsimhadeva, the Kazi was terrified and took shelter of the Lord's lotus feet. The Lord converted the Kazi and gave him love of Godhead. The Kazi declared that his descendants would not interfere with Vaisnavas. Two trees grow from his samadhi — a neem tree (representing Nimäi, Lord Caitanya) and a campaka tree (representing the Kazi, also called Campa) — over five hundred years old. The campaka blooms year-round even when out of season.

The place of the Kazi is compared to the city of Mathura, and the Kazi himself to Kamsa (who reappeared in Kali-yuga in a yavana family — *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Pramana-khanda 4.34).

**Sri Jagannatha Temple (Rajanpur)**  
Deities of Lord Jagannatha, Lord Baladeva, Subhadra-devi, and Sudarsana-cakra, more than five hundred years old. Established in Lord Caitanya's time by a devotee named Jagadisa Ganguli; miraculously recovered from a termite mound centuries later. ISKCON now manages this temple.

**Madana Gopala Temple (Belpukur)**  
The deity of Madana Gopala established and worshiped by Nilambara Cakravarti Thakura, the maternal grandfather of Lord Caitanya (father of Saci-devi). Nilambara Cakravarti moved here from Sylhet (Srihatta) approximately 1475–1490 CE, bringing his wife and daughter Saci-devi.

**Rama-tirtha (Rukumpur)**  
Where Lord Balarama killed the demon Kolasura. Members of the Yadu dynasty bathed here. Lord Ramacandra also visited during Treta-yuga. Lord Nityananda stayed here for a period after His marriage.

**Meghera Chara**  
Between Belpukur and Sonadanga: where Lord Caitanya, during nagara-sankirtana, waved His cloth to disperse a dark cloud that threatened to disrupt the kirtana — and the clouds immediately dispersed.

**Sri Nrsimha-ksetra / Narasimha Palli (in Godrumadvipa, but accessed from this area)**  
Where Brahma and the demigods made their houses and formed a village, all absorbed in worshiping Nrsimhadeva. Brahma-tila, Nrsimha-tila, Ganesa-tila, and Indra-tila mark the places where the demigods resided. Visvakarma had built hundreds of jeweled houses here, now only hillocks remain.

**Visrama-sthana**  
Where Lord Caitanya came to rest (visrama = rest) after travelling in kirtana. The Lord would rest here under the shade of trees.

**Prthu-kunda / Sarasanga / Ganganagara**  
Other sacred spots on or near Simantadvipa, described in the *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*: Ganganagara is where the Lord performed kirtana near the Ganga; Prthu-kunda is a holy pond; Sarasanga is a grove associated with Lord Caitanya's pastimes.

## 3. Godrumadvipa — Kirtanam (Chanting)

**Vraja equivalent:** Nandagrama (Nandisvarapura)  
**Location:** West of Simantadvipa, across the Sarasvati (Jalangi) River

Godrumadvipa corresponds to Nandagrama in Vraja, where Sri Gauranga performs the pastimes of a cowherd boy (*gopala*) in His eternal unmanifest lila — going with gopa friends, tending cows, eating yogurt and milk products in the afternoon.

### Places to Visit

**Surabhi-kunja (Gaudiya Matha)**  
The place where Indra and Surabhi performed bhajana and received darshana of Lord Gauranga. Located on the bank of the Jalangi River, this is non-different from Surabhivana in Vrindavana. Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura also stayed here for some years. The presiding deities are Sri Sri Radha-Krsna.

**Svänanda-sukhada-kunja (Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura's House)**  
The bhajana-kutira of Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura, named after the private grove of Lalita-sakhi in Vrindavana. While residing here, Bhaktivinoda Thakura wrote the *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* and discovered Lord Caitanya's appearance place at Yoga-pitha in Mayapur. His samadhi and Gaurakisora Dasa Babaji's bhajana-kutira are also here. From the verandah of his room, Bhaktivinoda Thakura saw visions of Lord Caitanya in kirtana and of the golden city of Mahaprabhu. The four great acaryas — Jagannatha Dasa Babaji, Gaurakisora Dasa Babaji, Bhaktivinoda Thakura, and Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati Thakura — are said to sometimes meet here to discuss the pastimes of Gaurahari.

**Suvarnavihara (Fort of King Suvarna Sena)**  
On the southern side of the Sarasvati River, the site of King Suvarna Sena's palace in Satya-yuga. Narada Muni visited the king here, gave extensive spiritual instructions, and fell into ecstatic dancing and chanting while speaking of Gauranga. The king subsequently had a dream-vision of Gaura-Gadadhara performing sankirtana, received a heavenly voice telling him he would become Buddhimanta Khan and serve the Lord, and began worshiping Gauranga.

**Sri Narasimha Palli / Harihara-ksetra**  
At Narasimha Palli (within Godrumadvipa) is a temple of Nrsimhadeva. This area is Harihara-ksetra, where the combined form of Hari and Hara (Krsna and Siva) resides. Here Lord Siva maintains the Maha-Varanasi within Navadvipa, non-different from the original Varanasi.

**Gauradaha (lake)**  
Where Lord Gauranga, while performing kirtana with cowherd people (gopas) and staying under a banyan tree, was approached by a gopa who told Him that the cows would not drink from this lake because a fearful crocodile lived there. Lord Gauranga began sankirtana loudly and the crocodile was so attracted that he came out and touched the Lord's lotus feet. The crocodile was immediately transformed into a *deva-sisu* (child of a demigod) — he had been cursed by Durvasa Muni for cutting off the sage's matted locks while he slept in Kamyavana. The curse was that he would remain a crocodile for four yugas, until *Saciprana-dhana* (Lord Caitanya) freed him through kirtana. From that day the lake was called Gauradaha, non-different from Kaliya-daha in Vrindavana.

**Ämra-ghata and Alakananda River**  
Sacred bathing spots along Godrumadvipa associated with the Lord's pastimes.

**Why the name "Godrumadvipa":**  
"Go" means cow and "druma" means tree. Mother Surabhi came here with Indra and worshiped Lord Gauranga under a banyan tree. She stays here eternally, serving the Lord's lotus feet. The cow-goddess (*surabhi*) under the tree (*druma*) gives this island its name.

## 4. Madhyadvipa — Smaranam (Remembering)

**Vraja equivalent:** No single specific forest; the island of meditation  
**Location:** South of Godrumadvipa

Madhyadvipa (the middle island) contains within it the places of Naimisaranya, Brahmana Puskara, Uccahatta/Kuruksetra, and Panca-veni.

### Places to Visit

**Hamsa-vahana Siva Temple**  
Dedicated to Lord Siva in His form as Hamsa-vahana — the one who rode Brahma's swan carrier to reach Naimisaranya in time to hear the *Gaura-bhagavata*. The deity of Hamsa-vahana Siva is said to become very hot because of Lord Siva's intense desire to see Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu. Approximately 50,000 people attend the annual festival here. The deity was kept in the Gomati River for most of the year and brought to the temple for three days around the full moon of Caitra (March–April).

**Naimisaranya**  
On the bank of the Gomati River within Madhyadvipa, non-different from the famous Naimisaranya of the Puranas. Here, in ancient times, the assembly of rsis headed by Saunaka gathered to hear the *Gaura-bhagavata* from Suta Gosvami. Lord Siva rode on Brahma's swan carrier to arrive here in time to hear the *gaura-katha*. He heard the glories of Lord Gauranga and His associates from the Puranas, surrounded by his followers from Kasi who danced and threw flowers in ecstasy. In *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* it is described: "Lord Gaurahari then went to Naimisa-tirtha. In Saptarsi-mandapa He danced with His associates and with Nityananda Avadhuta."

**Brahmana Puskara**  
South of Naimisaranya, non-different from Puskara-tirtha in western India. In Satya-yuga, the brahmin Divadasa traveled widely to visit holy places but was told in a dream to stay in Navadvipa. In his old age, unable to travel to Puskara-tirtha, he wept. Puskara-tirtha himself appeared in the form of a brahmin and told Divadasa to bathe in the nearby pond. When Divadasa did so, he received transcendental vision of Puskara-tirtha directly. Puskara-tirtha revealed: "I have always resided eternally here in Navadvipa-dhama. All tirthas reside here serving the dhama. By my expansion I manifest another form in western India — but whatever one gains by bathing a hundred times there, one gains by bathing here once." Puskara-tirtha then gave a prophecy about Lord Gauranga's coming appearance in Nadia.

**Uccahatta / Kuruksetra (Hattadanga)**  
South of Brahmana Puskara, non-different from Kuruksetra of Brahmavarta — a place of pilgrimage for the three worlds. All the demigods gathered here to discuss Gauranga's coming pastimes, opened a *hatta* (marketplace) of the holy name, performed *ucca-sankirtana* (loud chanting), and raised their hands praying for the Lord's swift descent. The demigods danced in various postures. Therefore the place is called Uccahatta. All the tirthas of Kuruksetra and Brahmavarta reside here permanently. Whatever benefit one derives by residing in Kuruksetra for one hundred years can be achieved by spending one night at this place.

**Panca-veni (Maha-Prayaga)**  
At the southern border of Madhyadvipa, before crossing the Ganga into Koladvipa, is Panca-veni — the confluence of five sacred rivers: the Mandakini, Alakananda, Bhagirathi, (hidden) Sarasvati, and Yamunä. The Bhogavati flows with the Yamuna on the western side, and Manasa-ganga also flows here. The rsis call this place Maha-Prayaga and perform millions of fire sacrifices here along with Lord Brahma. Anyone who bathes here will never take birth again in the material world. Demigods come here "on vacation." The *Navadvipa-bhava-taranga* states: "At this place the three streams of the River Ganga along with the Yamuna and Sarasvati rivers all join together, desiring to perform service to Lord Gaura. Here Gaurahari and His associates take bath, thereby purifying the tirtha from all the sins left by the people of Kali-yuga."

**Why the name "Madhyadvipa":**  
Because Lord Gauranga appeared here at midday (*madhyahna*), like the midday sun, when He gave darshana to the Sapta-rsis. In Satya-yuga, the seven sages came here on Brahma's instruction, performed intense worship, and received the Lord's darshana in the Panca-tattva form at noon. They also asked for and received the Lord's blessing to witness the *nama-sankirtana-lila* in Nadia.

## 5. Koladvipa — Pada-sevanam (Serving the Lotus Feet)

**Vraja equivalent:** Bahulavana; Dvaraka-puri  
**Location:** Western bank of the Ganga, opposite Madhyadvipa; also known as Kuliya-pahada (mountain) and now as the town of Navadvipa

Koladvipa is where Lord Varahadeva appeared and where Lord Caitanya came after taking sannyasa on His way to Vrindavana, granting His mercy and forgiving the sinful inhabitants of Kuliya.

### Places to Visit

**Dhamesvara Temple (Mahaprabhu Bari)**  
Contains the deity worshiped for eighty years by Vishnupriya-devi, Lord Caitanya's second wife, after He took sannyasa. She was sixteen when He left and worshipped this deity until her disappearance at age ninety-six. This deity of Lord Caitanya has His arms extended lovingly toward His devotees. Wooden shoes of Lord Caitanya are also kept here. The family still worships the deity as their son-in-law.

**Porama Tala (Praudha Maya and Vrddha Siva Temple)**  
The temple of Vrddha (Ksetra-pala) Siva and Praudha Maya-devi — the eternal protectors of Navadvipa-dhama. Praudha Maya is Yoga-maya (non-different from Paurnamasi in Vraja), who covers the dhama from non-devotees and reveals it to devotees. Vrddha Siva is the Ksetra-pala (guardian of the holy place). Originally worshiped in Mayapur, this deity was moved to the western bank when the Ganga shifted and flooded that part of Mayapur.

**Sri Jagannatha Dasa Babaji Maharaja's Samadhi and Bhajana-kutira**  
Jagannatha Dasa Babaji Maharaja was considered the most advanced rasika Vaisnava in both Vraja-mandala, Gaura-mandala, and Jagannatha Puri in his time. At Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura's request in 1893, he confirmed the discovery of Lord Caitanya's birthplace at Yoga-pitha in Mayapur — miraculously jumping fifteen feet into the air while chanting and dancing despite being so old he had to be carried in a basket.

**The Appearance of Lord Varahadeva:**  
In Satya-yuga, a brahmin named Vasudeva resided here, a great devotee of Lord Varahadeva. After intense worship and weeping, Lord Varahadeva appeared to him in the form of a boar as tall as a mountain, decorated with jewellery. He told Vasudeva: "When Kali-yuga comes, My manifested pastimes will take place in Navadvipa. This is My dearest dhama, kept very secret. You are very fortunate to serve Me here. You will take birth here again during My manifested pastimes and see the beautiful form of Gauranga and His great sankirtana pastimes." This raised land is non-different from Giri Govardhana in Vrindavana. The island is named "Koladvipa" because Lord Varahadeva (*kola* = boar) appeared here.

**Samudragarh**  
At the southeastern boundary of Koladvipa, at the confluence of the Ganga and the ocean. Non-different from Dvaraka-puri. Here, King Samudra Sena — a great devotee of Lord Krsna — deliberately provoked a battle with Bhimasena so that Bhima would call upon Krsna, giving the king an opportunity to see the Lord. Lord Krsna appeared on the battlefield (invisible to all except Samudra Sena) in His youthful form with clouds-like complexion, yellow dhoti, and jewellery. The king fainted in ecstasy, and upon reviving asked Krsna to also show the Gauranga form — which Krsna did, appearing in golden form performing mahä-sankirtana. Meanwhile the ocean itself came to Navadvipa to witness Gauranga's pastimes, flowing under the shelter of the Ganga.

**Campahaöti (Campahatta)**  
South of Samudragarh. In Satya-yuga, an elderly brahmin worshiper of Krsna here saw a campaka-colored golden form appear within the blue form of Krsna while he was performing puja. The brilliance of the golden form was like the gold of the campaka flower. He began announcing the coming of the *gaura-avatara* like someone making announcements in a *hatta* (marketplace), thus this became known as Campakahatti (later Campahaöti). Lord Gauranga also came here during His manifest pastimes and performed kirtana. A key temple here is the Gaura-Gadadhara Gaudiya Matha, with the original life-sized Gaura-Gadadhara deities installed and worshiped by Dvija Vaninatha (the younger brother of Gadadhara Pandita), over five hundred years old.

**Jayadeva's Place (within Campahaöti area)**  
Near Campahaöti is the place where Jayadeva Gosvami — author of the *Gita-Govinda* — lived. Lord Nityanananda described the appearance of Lord Gauranga to the poet Jayadeva. When Sri Jiva heard this, he immediately started rolling on the ground in ecstasy, crying, "All glories to poet Jayadeva! All glories to Padmavati. All glories to Sri Gita-Govinda!"

## 6. Rtudvipa — Arcanam (Worship)

**Vraja equivalent:** Radha-kunda and Syama-kunda in Vraja  
**Location:** Northeast of Campahaöti; the island of Ratupura

Rtudvipa corresponds to the most intimate sacred sites of Vraja — Radha-kunda and Syama-kunda. The six seasons reside here in their personal forms, anticipating the Lord's appearance.

### Places to Visit

**Radha-kunda and Syama-kunda**  
Lord Nityanananda pointed out Radha-kunda and Syama-kunda to Sri Jiva in Rtudvipa. In this place, Lord Gauranga along with His devotees would perform nama-sankirtana every afternoon. Lord Nityanananda declared: "There is no place equal to this in the three worlds. This is the best spot for devotees to perform bhajana. Whoever resides here will get love of Godhead and his heart will be pacified." The Bhaktivedanta Swami Charity Trust has identified and acquired the land where these sacred kundas are located.

Lord Gauranga would sometimes remember Krsna's Radha-kunda pastimes while doing sankirtana here, and would cry remembering Radha-kunda and other sacred places.

**Vidyanagara (Sarada-pitha / Seat of Sarasvati)**  
The most important educational center in the universe, where all knowledge (*vidya*) originates. Lord Brahma originally taught the demigods and sages here. At the time of annihilation, Lord Matsya resides here holding all the Vedas. All the great philosophical systems were revealed here:
- *Ramayana* (Valmiki, by Narada's mercy)
- *Ayur-veda* (Dhanvantari)
- *Archery* (Visvamitra)
- *Vedic mantras* (Saunaka Rsi)
- *Tantra* (Mahadeva Siva)
- *Four Vedas* (Brahma, at the rsis' request)
- *Sankhya* (Kapila Muni)
- *Nyaya-tarka* (Gautama Rsi)
- *Vaisesika* (Kanada Muni)
- *Yoga-sastra* (Patanjali)
- *Mimamsa* (Jaimini)
- *Puranas* (Vedavyasa)
- *Pancatantra* (Narada and four others)

**Brhaspati appeared as Vasudeva Sarvabhauma:**  
Knowing the Lord would perform His *vidya-lila* here, Brhaspati descended as Sarvabhauma Bhattacarya. Before the Lord's birth, Sarvabhauma set up schools here, then prudently left for Nilacala-dhama (Puri), arranging the divine pastime of Mahaprabhu's later conversion of Sarvabhauma from Mayavada. Lord Caitanya would come to Vidyanagara and defeat all Sarvabhauma's disciples through *parihasa* (playful joking). On one occasion, when Lord Caitanya was traveling to Vrindavana for the first time, He stopped here for five days at the house of Vidya-vacaspati. Hundreds of thousands of people came to see Him, requiring jungles to be cleared and many boats to transport visitors.

**Srila Saravabhauma Bhattacarya's Birthplace Temple** — here one can take darshana of Sri Sri Radha-Syamasundara and Gauranga Mahaprabhu.

**Vidyanagara Gaudiya Matha** — contains beautiful life-sized neem Gaura-Nitai deities worshiped by Vidya-vacaspati (Sarvabhauma's brother).

**"Pen trees" (kalam trees)** — trees said to have grown from pens Nimäi planted in the ground; they do not belong to any known species.

**Why the name "Rtudvipa":**  
The six seasons (*rtu*) reside here in personal forms, constantly meditating on and worshiping the Lord. They discussed among themselves about the Lord's coming appearance, with Vasanta (spring) being especially joyful because the Lord would appear in the spring season (Phalguna-purnima). Therefore this island is called Rtudvipa.

---

## 7. Jahnudvipa — Vandanam (Praying)

**Vraja equivalent:** Bhadravana  
**Location:** North of Vidyanagara; Jannagara / Jannadvipa

Jahnudvipa is where the Ganga became known as Jahnavi, and where Bhismadeva studied and performed austerities.

### Places to Visit

**Jahnu Muni's Asrama**  
The main site of Jahnudvipa, identifiable today by a large banyan tree that marks the area of Jahnu Muni's hermitage. Jahnu Muni performed tapasya here. Once, while performing *acamana*, his cup and spoon were carried away by the powerful current of the Bhagirathi. Enraged, Jahnu Muni drank the entire Ganga. King Bhagiratha then worshiped Jahnu Muni to please him, whereupon the muni released the Ganga from his thigh (some accounts say from his ear). Since then the Ganga has also been called Jahnavi — the daughter of Jahnu.

**Lord Gauranga's Darshana to Jahnu Muni:**  
Jahnu Muni, knowing of the Lord's coming appearance in Kali-yuga, meditated intensely on Lord Gauranga. In his meditation, he first saw Lord Krsna in the tribhanga form with peacock feather, playing the flute — then saw the same Lord transform into a young sannyasi with a danda and kamandalu, head shaved, wearing saffron cloth, with radiance defeating millions of suns. Opening his eyes, he saw the Lord standing before him in golden form with curling hair and dazzling ornaments. Jahnu Muni fell at the Lord's lotus feet and washed them with his tears. The Lord embraced him, said "All your desires will be fulfilled," and disappeared. Jahnu Muni thereafter chanted the glories of Nadia-candra day and night, his body soaked with tears of ecstasy.

**Bhisma-tila**  
A hillock marking where Bhismadeva was born from his mother Ganga-devi and where he later came to visit his maternal grandfather Jahnu Muni. Jahnu Muni kept Bhisma at his asrama for a long time, teaching him dharma, political science, knowledge of the Supreme Lord, and devotional service. Bhisma then passed this knowledge on to King Yudhisthira at Kuruksetra. While staying in Navadvipa, Bhisma received *bhakti* and therefore became famous as a great Vaisnava. While lying on his deathbed of arrows at Kuruksetra, Bhisma glorified Lord Caitanya in the *Visnu-sahasra-nama-stotra*:

> *"suvarëa-varëo hemäìgo / varäìgaç candanäìgadé / sannyäsa-kåc chamaù çänto / niñöhä-çänti-paräyaëaù"*  
> "In His early pastimes He appears as a householder with a golden complexion... In His later pastimes He accepts the sannyasa order, and He is equipoised and peaceful."

## 8. Modadrumadvipa — Dasyam (Becoming a Servant)

**Vraja equivalent:** Bhandira-vana; non-different from Ayodhya  
**Location:** Mamgachi (Maugachi); northwest of Jahnudvipa

Modadrumadvipa is where Lord Ramacandra visited with Sita and Lakshmana during Treta-yuga and revealed to Sita His coming appearance as Gauranga in Kali-yuga.

### Places to Visit

**Rama-vata (Banyan Tree of Ramacandra)**  
Under a great banyan tree here, Lord Ramacandra, Sita, and Lakshmana took shelter during Their forest journey. Lord Ramacandra smiled upon seeing the effulgence of Navadvipa and told Sita a confidential secret: He would appear here in Kali-yuga as Gauranga, in a golden-yellow form, perform wonderful sankirtana pastimes, marry twice, and finally take sannyasa. Sita objected ("Why will You make Your mother cry?"), and Rama pacified Her by showing Her a vision of the Lord's Nadia pastimes — after which She could not control Herself. Lord Ramacandra then said: "That is why I was smiling — remembering all this." He declared: "This Navadvipa is My dear place — even Ayodhya is not equal to it." With the coming of Kali-yuga this tree disappeared from material vision but remains in its unmanifest form.

The island is named "Modadruma" because everyone's pleasure (*moda*) was increased here under this tree (*druma*).

**Sadananda's Place:**  
In Mamgachi there was a brahmin worshiper of Lord Ramacandra (identified as Guhaka-raja reborn) named Sadananda Vipra Bhattacarya. He was present in Jagannatha Misra's house at the moment of Lord Gauranga's birth and recognized — from the demigods' presence — that his Lord had taken birth. Later, in meditation, he saw Lord Gauranga on a jeweled throne surrounded by demigods with Brahma at their head waving camaras, then saw the scene transform into Lord Ramacandra with Sita, Lakshmana, and Hanuman. He understood that Gauranga is non-different from Ramacandra, became a regular visitor to Mayapur, and participated in the sankirtana movement.

**Vrindavana Dasa Thakura's Sripata**  
Srila Vrindavana Dasa Thakura, the author of *Sri Caitanya-bhagavata* and considered an incarnation of Vedavyasa, took birth here in Modadrumadvipa. He was the disciple of Nityananda Prabhu. His mother Narayani-devi (niece of Srivasa Pandita) received the remnants of Lord Caitanya's food directly from the Lord's hand when she was four years old. The deities that Vrindavana Dasa Thakura worshiped — Nitai-Gauranga and Sri Jagannatha — are still worshiped here.

**Vasudeva Datta's Sripata**  
The residence of Vasudeva Datta, one of the most intimate associates of Lord Caitanya, famous for offering to take on the karma of all conditioned souls so that the Lord could deliver them without their individual repentance. The Madana-Gopala deities he worshiped are now kept at Saranga Murari's Sripata.

**Saranga Murari Sripata**  
The sripata of Saranga Thakura (also known as Sringapani, Sarngadhara, Saranga Thakura), who performed bhajana in Mamgachi. One morning he resolved to accept as his disciple the first person he saw. When he went for his morning bath in the Ganga, a dead boy's body floated by and touched his feet — the boy immediately came back to life. This boy, named Murari, became Saranga Thakura's disciple (hence Saranga *Murari* Sripata). A large bakula tree at this temple is said to have been present during Saranga Thakura's time; Lord Caitanya is said to have embraced this tree when it was dying, and it immediately came back to life with green leaves and fragrant flowers. The deity of Sri Gopinatha worshiped by Saranga Thakura is still worshiped here.

**Arka-tila**  
In the *Navadvipa-bhava-taranga*, Bhaktivinoda Thakura describes: "After crossing the Viraja River and leaving behind Brahmanagara I will climb upon Arka-tila. There I will worship Sri Gaurahari solitarily with great attention. Arkadeva (the sun-god), being merciful upon me, will appear in front of me with his reddish complexion, wearing saffron-colored cloth, his whole body smeared with sandalwood and tears flowing from his eyes while he chants the holy name of Gaurahari." The sun-god tells the devotee: "By my blessings you will develop devotion unto Sri Krsna. Please always come to me chanting the nectarine holy name and thus give me pleasure."

**Vaikunthapura**  
Within the broader Modadrumadvipa area, where Lord Narayana (the ruler of Vaikuñöha) resides, served by His energies Sri, Bhu, and Nila. From this spiritual abode, a radiance emanates that is the Brahman effulgence. Narada Muni once saw Lord Narayana manifesting here as Gaurasundara and remained here for some time. Madhvacarya came here, took darshana of Lord Narayana, and received his mission. The four sampradaya acaryas received their inspiration and instructions through Navadvipa.

**Mahatpura**  
In the northwestern part of Modadrumadvipa (now called Matapura). The five Pandavas and Draupadi came here during their forest exile (*vana-vasa*). King Yudhisthira, sensing the extraordinary nature of this place, meditated throughout the night and received a dream-vision of Lord Balarama, who told him: "This Navadvipa is where Sri Krsna will appear in Kali-yuga in a brahmin family." When the Pandavas came to Navadvipa, they had a vision of Krsna and Balarama, who revealed the Gauranaga form to Yudhisthira. Because Yudhisthira Maharaja was the greatest among all *mahats* (great persons) and stayed here, it is called Mahatpura. Five banyan trees provided shade here; there was also a Yudhisthira-tila. By the Lord's desire, all these places are now hidden.

## 9. Rudradvipa — Sakhyam (Being a Friend)

**Vraja equivalent:** Vrindavana / Pulina (on the western bank of the Yamuna); Kailasa is its effulgence  
**Location:** Eastern and western banks of the Ganga; the western portion is now on the eastern side due to the river's shifting course

Rudradvipa is where the eleven Rudras reside and dance in bliss. It contains Sri Pulina — the transcendental sandy bank where Lord Gauranga performed the rasa-dance pastimes in the mood of Lord Krsna.

### Places to Visit

**Sankarapura**  
A village in Rudradvipa on the bank of the Ganga. When Sankaracarya was traveling to conquer all directions, he came to conquer Navadvipa. Although internally a great Vaisnava, externally he was spreading Advaita-vada (monism) as the servant of maya and by the Lord's order. Lord Rudra, whose partial expansion Sankaracarya is, had been assigned by the Lord to preach mayavada philosophy in order to confuse atheists and lead them away from theism to a preliminary level. However, Navadvipa-dhama is so dear to the Lord that mayavada has no place here. Lord Gauranga appeared to Sankaracarya in a dream and told him sweetly: "My dear servant, by My order you are preaching mayavada very diligently, but this Navadvipa-dhama is very dear to Me and mayavada has no place here. You had better go somewhere else." Understanding the *navadvipa-tattva* through his dream, Sankaracarya left for other countries. This Rudradvipa is a place for all the Rudras, who gather to sing Gauranga's glories.

**Nidaya Ghata**  
Where Lord Caitanya crossed the River Ganga on His way to Katwa to accept sannyasa. He took sannyasa at the end of January 1510, just before His 25th birthday. His sannyasa name was Sri Krsna Caitanya Mahaprabhu. On that night He informed only Nityananda, Gadadhara, Saci-mata, Candrashekhara, and Mukunda. As He approached the gate to leave home forever, Mother Saci stood there like a statue, unable to speak, tears filling her eyes. The Lord circumambulated her three times, offered obeisance, took the dust from her feet, and left home forever. He then swam across the Ganga at this point and went to Katwa. "Nidaya" means "without mercy" — the residents of Navadvipa accused the Lord of being merciless for leaving them.

**Sri Pulina, Rasa-mandala, Dhira-samira**  
The most sacred spots on the western bank of the Ganga, opposite Mayapur. The *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* (Pramana-khanda 4.25–26) states:  

> *"Intelligent people say that Mayapura, identical to Gokula, lies on the eastern bank of the Ganga. On its western bank lies Vrindavana. In this Vrindavana there is a place called Rasa-sthali. On the west side of the Rasa-sthali is the holy place Dhira-samira where the wind blows very gently."*

**Rasa-mandala:** Where Lord Krsna performs the eternal rasa-dance with a billion gopis, and where Lord Gauranga came and entered maha-bhava while chanting the rasa-lila verses from *Srimad-Bhagavatam*. Dundubhi drums sounded in heaven, flowers showered down, and sages chanted Chandogya and Veda-mantras. The Lord remained unconscious in maha-bhava for four dandas (approximately 96 minutes), until devotees chanted *gaura-gita* into His ear.

**Dhira-samira:** On the western side of the Rasa-mandala, a spot with a gentle, cooling breeze — non-different from the Dhira-samira on the Yamuna bank in Vrindavana. The Ganga here is, in its transcendental reality, the Yamuna.

**Sri Pulina:** The sandy banks (*pulina*) of the Ganga — a transcendental resting and playing spot. In Bhaktivinoda Thakura's *Navadvipa-bhava-taranga*, it is here that he receives the mercy of Ananga-manjari, is introduced to Rupa-manjari and Lalita-sakhi, and receives his eternal spiritual identity in the maidservant of Sri Radha's service.

**Why the name "Rudradvipa":**  
Rudradeva (Lord Siva) began dancing ecstatically here, chanting the glories of Lord Gauranga before His appearance in Nadia. He danced so powerfully that the earth shook at every step. The Lord appeared invisibly before Rudra to pacify him. Lord Rudra now sits here with his associates distributing the nectar of *gaura-katha*. Nila-lohita (the chief of the Rudras) dances here with great jubilation every day, and the demigods shower flowers from the sky in appreciation.
