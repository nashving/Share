## Overview

The *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* (by Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura), as well as the *Caitanya-bhagavata*, *Bhakti-ratnakara*, and the island booklets compiled from these sources, describe an extraordinary series of pastimes in which demigods, sages, and celestial beings came to Navadvipa-dhama — either long before the Lord's appearance, to perform worship and receive His darshana, or at the moment of His birth, or during His manifest pastimes. Each of the nine islands of Navadvipa-mandala preserves at least one such pastime.

A striking theme runs through all these accounts: the demigods themselves — including Brahma, Siva, Indra, and the Saptarsis — recognized their own tendency toward pride or forgetfulness and came to Navadvipa to beg for Lord Gauranga's mercy, so that they would not offend Him when He manifested in Kali-yuga.

## Lord Brahma — Antardvipa

**Location:** Antardvipa (central island), the place called Atopur / Brahmapura  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Pramana-khanda 4.32; island booklet

Five thousand years ago (i.e., after the end of Dvapara-yuga), Brahma came to Antardvipa to worship Lord Gauranga in preparation for Kali-yuga. The background is the well-known Brahma-vimohana pastime: Brahma had stolen the calves and cowherd boys of Vrindavana to test Lord Krsna. After being defeated and humbled, Brahma felt remorse and worried he might again commit offense when Krsna descended as Gauranga. Thinking, "I am very proud — unless the Lord removes this pride, I will fall again," Brahma came to Atopur and performed intense bhajana.

Lord Gauranga appeared before Brahma in a magnificent golden form, with eyes extending to His ears, arms reaching to His thighs, and a smile that attracted the whole universe. Brahma fainted at His lotus feet. When the Lord placed His feet on Brahma's head, Brahma regained transcendental awareness and offered this prayer:

> "I myself, Lord Siva, and the demigods headed by Indra are Your servants, as the scriptures describe. But we are not so fortunate as to be Your pure devotees, and thus maya covers us. I have passed the first half of my life-span. I pray that You remove my pride and allow me to chant Your glories as Haridasa in a low-caste family, without any pride."

The Lord replied: "When I manifest My pastimes in Nadia, you will take birth in the house of yavanas [mlecchas], completely free from pride. You will be the great devotee Haridasa. You will chant three hundred thousand holy names daily, and at the time of leaving your body you will have My darshana. By the power of your sadhana you will enter My eternal abode Navadvipa-dhama."

The island Antardvipa is named for this pastime — "antara" signifying the inner surrender (*atma-nivedanam*) that Brahma demonstrated here.

## Sapta-rsis (Seven Sages) — Madhyadvipa

**Location:** Madhyadvipa (Majida village)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda (Nityanananda Prabhu to Sri Jiva); *Bhakti-ratnakara* (Isana Thakura to Srinivaas Acarya); island booklet

In Satya-yuga, the Sapta-rsis (seven great sages) visited their father Brahma in Brahmaloka. They found him chanting the holy name of Gauranga. They asked how to obtain *gaura-prema*, and Brahma instructed them: "Go to Navadvipa and chant the glories of Lord Gauranga. By the mercy of the dhama you will get sadhu-sanga, and from that you will develop krsna-prema."

The sages came to Madhyadvipa and performed intense worship — stopping eating and sleeping, constantly chanting Gaura's name, and crying: "O Gaurahari! Give us Your darshana. We have been offenders by not following true dharma. Now we understand that bhakti alone is the essence of everything."

At midday — as brilliant as the midday sun — Lord Gauranga appeared before the Sapta-rsis along with the Panca-tattva, wearing a garland of mallika flowers, a brahminical thread, and cloth folded thrice. The sages were overwhelmed, trembling, offering prostrated obeisances again and again, lamenting that they lacked thousands of eyes to see the Lord.

The Lord said: "All your desires will be fulfilled. Very soon in the city of Nadia during My manifested pastimes, all of you will witness the nama-sankirtana-lila. Keep this subject matter hidden for now. Go to Kumara-hatta and perform krsna-bhajana there."

The seven hillocks (tilas) where the sages sat are still visible in the area, arranged in the same formation as the Sapta-rsi constellation in the night sky.

Because Lord Gauranga appeared at midday (*madhyahna*), like the midday sun, this island is called Madhyadvipa.

## Lord Siva and His Followers — Rudradvipa

**Location:** Rudradvipa (Radupura / Rudrapura)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet

In anticipation of Lord Gauranga's appearance in Nadia, Lord Siva (Rudradeva) became overwhelmed with ecstasy and began dancing while chanting Gauranga's glories. His dancing was so powerful that the earth shook at every step. From heaven, the demigods began showering flowers, declaring: "Now all living entities will be free from all suffering. Before the appearance of the Lord, Rudradeva is chanting the glories of His birth — this means the Lord will appear in Nadia very soon!"

All the demigods in heaven also began to dance.

Lord Gauranga, invisible to others, appeared before Rudradeva to pacify him, saying: "I will fulfill your desire. I will appear very soon along with My associates."

Hearing this, Rudra offered many prayers and circumambulated the Lord. After the Lord disappeared, Rudra was distressed in separation, but by the Lord's mercy he regained composure.

Lord Rudra (Nila-lohita) and his eleven associates continue to reside in Rudradvipa, constantly chanting Gauranga's glories and dancing in bliss, while demigods shower flowers in appreciation.

## Lord Siva at Naimisaranya — Madhyadvipa

**Location:** Naimisaranya (in Madhyadvipa), on the bank of the Gomati River  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; *Navadvipa-bhava-taranga*; island booklet

At Naimisaranya within Navadvipa, the assembly of rsis headed by Saunaka Rsi gathered to hear the *Gaura-bhagavata* from Suta Gosvami. While this recitation was taking place, Lord Siva, residing in Kailasa, deeply desired to hear the *gaura-katha* but realized his bull carrier (Nandi) was too slow to get him there in time.

Lord Siva therefore requested the use of Lord Brahma's swan carrier (*hamsa-vahana*). Riding the swan swiftly, he arrived at Naimisaranya in time and heard the glories of Lord Gauranga and His associates from the Puranas.

All the followers of Lord Siva, the inhabitants of Kasi, surrounded Panchanana (five-faced Siva) and danced ecstatically, throwing flowers, chanting the holy name of Gauranga.

A deity at the Hamsa-vahana Siva Temple in Madhyadvipa commemorates this pastime. The linga of Hamsa-vahana Siva becomes hot because of Lord Siva's intense desire to see Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu.

*Navadvipa-bhava-taranga* describes: "In order to hear the narratives about Lord Gaura, Siva and the other demigods quickly mount Brahma's swan-carrier and make haste to come."

## Parvati-devi — Simantadvipa

**Location:** Simantadvipa (Simuliya-grama)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda 4.33; *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet

In Kailasa, Lord Siva began dancing ecstatically, chanting the names of all the devotees who would appear in Navadvipa in Kali-yuga. When Parvati asked him what "Gauranga" was — this name she had not heard before — Siva revealed the secret:

> "During Kali-yuga, Lord Sri Krsna, accepting the mood of Radharani, will take birth as Gauranga in Mayapur from the womb of Saci-devi. He will be mad with chanting the holy name, and will distribute the wealth of prema to everyone without considering who is qualified or not."

Parvati immediately came to Simantadvipa, on the southern bank of the Ganga, and performed intense worship — chanting the holy name of Gaura with great love.

After some days, Lord Gauranga appeared before her in golden form, tall and beautiful, with gently curling locks, cloth folded thrice, and a flower garland. Parvati fell at His feet and told Him her "pitiful situation" — that as the power of maya she was always distant from Krsna, unable to witness His pastimes.

The Lord replied: "Dear Parvati, you are not different from Me. You are My energy. As My svarupa-sakti you are Radhika. In Vraja you reside eternally as Paurnamasi. In Navadvipa you remain as Paudha Maya along with Ksetra-pala Mahadeva." He granted all her desires.

Parvati took the dust of the Lord's lotus feet and placed it on her *simanta* (the central parting of a woman's hair), from which this island takes its name.

Parvati now resides in Simantadvipa as Simantini-devi in one form, and as Paudha Maya-devi in Mayapur in another form, for the pleasure of Lord Gauranga.

## Indra and Surabhi — Godrumadvipa

**Location:** Godrumadvipa (Gadigacha / Surabhi-kunja)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

After the Govardhana-lila in Dvapara-yuga, Indra fell at Lord Krsna's feet, was forgiven, but remained fearful. He approached mother Surabhi and confessed: "I fear I may again become bewildered when Krsna appears in Nadia in Kali-yuga and commit further offense at His lotus feet."

Surabhi replied: "Let us go to Navadvipa-dhama. There we will worship Nimäi." Indra and Surabhi came to Godrumadvipa and engaged in the worship of Lord Gauranga through intense bhajana — chanting the holy name with tears.

Lord Gauranga manifested before them with a sweet smile, saying: "I know your desire. Very soon I will manifest My pastimes in Nadia. During this pastime you will serve Me here, so that you will not get caught by the net of maya anymore."

Surabhi-devi stays eternally in Godrumadvipa under the banyan tree, constantly serving the lotus feet of Lord Gauranga. Because mother Surabhi (*go*) resides here under a tree (*druma*), the island is called Godrumadvipa.

The place where Indra and Surabhi performed their bhajana is known as Surabhi-kunja, where a Gaudiya Matha temple now stands.

## Brhaspati Appears as Sarvabhauma — Rtudvipa (Vidyanagara)

**Location:** Vidyanagara, within Rtudvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; *Bhakti-ratnakara*; *Caitanya-bhagavata*; island booklet

In the assembly of the demigods, Brhaspati (the preceptor of the gods) became filled with ecstasy upon contemplating that the Lord would perform His *vidya-lila* (educational pastimes) in Navadvipa. He declared: "In this Kali-yuga, the Lord will appear in the city of Nadia in the house of Jagannatha Misra. In His incarnation as Gauranga He will be the most learned. I cannot control myself — I must go to Navadvipa immediately and worship the Lord there upon His appearance."

Knowing the Lord would enjoy *vidya-rasa*, Brhaspati and his associates left Indra's court (*indra-sabha*) and appeared on earth to prepare the ground.

Brhaspati took birth as Vasudeva Sarvabhauma Bhattacarya in Vidyanagara. He established schools (*tolas*) and spread knowledge throughout the area. But thinking, "In the future, due to the influence of dry knowledge, I may lose Gauranga," he prudently left Nadia before the Lord's appearance and went to Nilacala-dhama — thereby arranging the divine pastime of Mahaprabhu's later visit to Puri and the conversion of Sarvabhauma from mayavada.

The *Caitanya-bhagavata* confirms: "For this pastime, the most worshipable Brhaspati, along with his disciples, appeared in Navadvipa."

## All Demigods at Antardvipa (Navadvipa General Glorification)

**Location:** Antardvipa / all of Navadvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda, General Glorification; island booklet

The general glorification of Navadvipa states:

> "Those people who always reside in Gaura-mandala are the most fortunate in the world. The demigods in the heavenly planets see them as having beautiful four-handed forms of blackish complexion. Within the sixteen krosas of Navadvipa-dhama, the numerous residents — golden in complexion — constantly engage in congregational chanting of the Lord's holy names. Brahma and other demigods come from outer space and worship them in various ways."

Lord Brahma himself prays: "When will I become so fortunate as to take birth as a blade of grass in Navadvipa-dhama? Then I will receive the dust from the feet of the devotees who are engaged in serving the lotus feet of Sri Gauranga."

The devas, rsis, and Rudras always reside in different parts of Navadvipa, engaged in austerities. As long as bodily consciousness is not given up, however, they cannot obtain Nityananda's mercy.

## All Demigods at Uccahatta — Madhyadvipa

**Location:** Uccahatta (Hattadanga), within Madhyadvipa, on the border of Nadia  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet

The *Bhakti-ratnakara* describes that at Uccahatta (also called Kuruksetra / Brahmavarta), all the demigods including Indra gathered, absorbed in discussing the pastimes and associates of Gauranga Mahaprabhu. Their conversation ranged over the following themes:

- "This Kali-yuga is blessed because Sri Krsna Caitanya Mahaprabhu will appear, along with Lord Nityananda and Advaitacarya."
- "Mahaprabhu has unlimited associates. Who can count them? All His associates are oceans of mercy."
- "Mahaprabhu, along with His associates, will be mad with nama-sankirtana and will make the whole world mad."
- "There will be unlimited auspiciousness. Only the most fortunate people will see those wonderful pastimes. If all of us could take birth in Nadia, our great sadness would be removed."
- "Of course all of us will take birth here. We will see the pastimes of the Lord to our heart's content."

Speaking in this way, the demigods opened a marketplace (*hatta*) there. At this raised (*ucca*) place they performed *ucca-sankirtana* (loud congregational chanting). All of them raised their hands, praying: "O Lord! Do not delay Your descent." They danced very joyfully in different postures while chanting the holy name.

Therefore this place is called Uccahatta. Here all the demigods and all the holy tirthas reside, continuously performing *gaura-sankirtana*. Sometimes the present-day residents of this area can hear that music.

## Demigods Shower Flowers at the Rasa-mandala — Rudradvipa

**Location:** Rasa-mandala and Pulina, western bank of the Ganga (Rudradvipa)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Pramana-khanda 4.49–54; island booklet

The *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya* Pramana-khanda describes Lord Gauranga visiting the Rasa-sthali (Rasa-mandala) on the western bank of the Bhagirathi River:

> "Sriman Mahaprabhu Gaurasundara saw Vrindavana on the bank of the Ganga. Along with His associates the Lord observed the Rasa-sthali there and started to chant the verses of Srimad-Bhagavatam relating to the rasa-lila. By remembering rasa-lila at this place, Mahaprabhu entered into the state of maha-bhava. At that time *dundubhi* drums were sounded in heaven and flowers showered down. The sages were chanting the Chandogya and Veda-mantras."

The Lord was so absorbed in maha-bhava that He remained unconscious for four dandas (approximately 96 minutes), until the devotees chanted Gaura-gita (songs about Gaura) into His ear, causing Him to awaken, loudly chanting "Hare Krsna! Hare Krsna!"

## Demigods at the Lord's Appearance — Modadrumadvipa

**Location:** Modadrumadvipa (Mamgachi)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

When Lord Gauranga appeared as the son of Jagannatha Misra and Saci-devi in Mayapur, all the demigods descended to Jagannatha Misra's house to take darshana of the infant form of the Lord.

The devotee Sadananda Vipra Bhattacarya (from Mamgachi / Modadrumadvipa), who was a worshiper of Lord Ramacandra in his previous identity as Guhaka-raja, was present in the house of Jagannatha Misra that day. He recognized the demigods' presence and understood that his Lord had taken birth.

When he returned home and meditated on his worshipable Ramacandra, he saw a vision:

> "Lord Gauranga was seated on a throne, surrounded by the demigods headed by Lord Brahma, who were waving camaras. Then Sadananda saw Ramacandra, green as grass. On Rama's right was Lakshmana; on His left was Sita; and in front was Hanuman."

By seeing this, Sadananda understood that Gauranga was none other than his worshipable Lord Ramacandra. He became a regular visitor to Mayapur and later participated in the sankirtana movement, dancing and chanting Gaura's name.

## Brahma and Demigods at Lord's Appearance — Koladvipa

**Location:** Navadvipa, general (described in Koladvipa chapter)  
**Source:** *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet (Koladvipa, Samudragarh section)

The *Bhakti-ratnakara* describes the moment of Lord Gauranga's birth as seen by the ocean (Samudra) through the eyes of Ganga-devi:

> "The rsis were chanting different types of prayers. Brahma and all the demigods were showering flowers. As soon as the Lord appeared as the son of Saci-devi, the news spread all over the universe."

The ocean then came, under the shelter of the Ganga, to Navadvipa every day to witness the pastimes of Sri Gauracandra.

## Visvakarma Prepares Navadvipa — Simantadvipa (Sasthi-tirtha)

**Location:** Simantadvipa, Sasthi-tirtha area  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

When the demigods heard that Mahaprabhu would descend at Navadvipa, Visvakarma (the divine architect) came to Nadia. He saw that it would be hard to get water on the roads where the Lord would do kirtana in the future. Therefore, in a single night, Visvakarma dug out sixty (sasthi) wide ponds — the last one at the Kazi's village.

This is commemorated at Sasthi-tirtha (also near Simantadvipa). These ponds, dug by the divine architect Visvakarma, facilitated the Lord's future nagara-sankirtana processions.

## Brahma and Demigods at Nrsimha-ksetra — Godrumadvipa

**Location:** Nrsimha-ksetra / Narasimha Palli, within Godrumadvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

Lord Nityanananda told Sri Jiva: "Brahma and the other demigods made their houses here and formed a village [at Nrsimha-ksetra]. This village was established on tilas (hillocks) here on the bank of the Mandakini River, and all the residents were absorbed in the worship of Nrsimhadeva."

Among the tilas visible were Brahma-tila, Nrsimha-tila, Ganesa-tila, and Indra-tila. Although Visvakarma had built hundreds of jeweled houses there, in time they were destroyed and only the hillocks remain as markers of where the demigods resided.

## Bhisma Receives Darshana of Lord Caitanya — Jahnudvipa

**Location:** Jahnudvipa (Jannagara), at Bhisma-tila  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

Bhismadeva, the son of King Santanu and Ganga-devi, studied in the heavenly planets under Brhaspati, and after completing his studies, came to visit his maternal grandfather Jahnu Muni in Navadvipa. Jahnu Muni instructed him that Lord Caitanya would appear in Navadvipa-dhama and gave him teachings on religious principles, political science, the Supreme Lord, and devotional service.

Bhismadeva performed austerities in Jahnudvipa and received darshana of Lord Caitanya. The Lord informed Bhisma that he would attain Navadvipa in the future and that he would teach King Yudhisthira the highest knowledge at the time of death.

While lying on his deathbed of arrows at Kuruksetra, Bhisma glorified Lord Caitanya in the well-known verse from the *Visnu-sahasra-nama-stotra*:

> "In His early pastimes He appears as a householder with a golden complexion. His limbs are beautiful, and His body, smeared with the pulp of sandalwood, seems like molten gold. In His later pastimes He accepts the sannyasa order, and He is equipoised and peaceful. He is the highest abode of peace and devotion, for He silences the impersonalist nondevotees." (*Visnu-sahasra-nama-stotra*, quoted in the Jahnudvipa booklet)

## Narada Muni — Multiple Locations

**Location:** Godrumadvipa (with King Suvarna Sena); Simantadvipa (Nrsimha-ksetra)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklets

Narada Muni appears in several pastimes connected with Navadvipa:

1. **With King Suvarna Sena (Godrumadvipa):** During Satya-yuga, Narada Muni visited King Suvarna Sena in Suvarnavihara (Godrumadvipa). He gave the king extensive instructions on the futility of material wealth, the nature of the soul, and the process of bhakti. While speaking to the king, Narada became so overwhelmed with ecstasy that he began dancing, chanting the holy name of Gauranga, playing his vina, and asking: "When will that most blessed Kali-yuga come?" The king subsequently had a dream-vision of Gaura-Gadadhara and received a heavenly voice (*daiva-vani*) telling him he would take birth as Buddhimanta Khan and serve the Lord.

2. **At Nrsimha-ksetra / Sarada-pitha (Rtudvipa):** The *Pancatantra* (a collection of works by five sages including Narada) was composed/revealed at Vidyanagara, described as *Sarada-pitha* — the seat of Sarasvati. Narada Muni is described as one of the sages who manifested knowledge at this holy site.

## Sarasvati-devi — Rtudvipa (Vidyanagara)

**Location:** Vidyanagara / Sarada-pitha, within Rtudvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

Lord Nityanananda told Sri Jiva: "After the annihilation, when the time of creation again arrives, Brahma becomes very fearful witnessing the vast annihilation. At that time the Lord shows him His mercy. Here Brahma offered prayers to the Lord and just as he opened his mouth to begin his offering, Sarasvati-devi appeared from his tongue in her most beautiful form. Then, by the energy of Sarasvati, Brahma offered a prayer (*stava*) to Lord Sri Krsna with great happiness."

Sarasvati-devi resides at Vidyanagara (*Sarada-pitha*) undoubtedly. The six principal branches of philosophical knowledge — Sankhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisesika, Mimamsa, and Vedanta — were all manifested here by various rsis (Kapila, Patanjali, Gautama, Kanada, Jaimini, Vedavyasa). All these philosophies serve as servants of bhakti here in the eternal dhama.

## The Srutis (Vedic Hymns Personified) — Rtudvipa (Vidyanagara)

**Location:** Vidyanagara, within Rtudvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

In the garden at Vidyanagara, the Upanisads (personified Srutis) worshiped Sri Gauranga for a long time. Sri Gaurahari responded through an unseen sound vibration, informing them:

> "None of you will attain Me in this form of Sruti. When you appear as My associates during My manifested pastimes, then you will all see Me. At that time all of you will glorify My qualities."

Hearing this, the Srutis kept quiet and stayed secretly at Vidyanagara, waiting for a suitable time. This is why the Vedic wisdom "stays concealed" until Mahaprabhu's *gaura-lila* reveals its inner meaning.

---

## Visnu Svami Blessed by Rudra — Rudradvipa

**Location:** Rudradvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

One time Sri Visnu Svami (the acarya of the Visnu Svami-sampradaya) went travelling to defeat opposing philosophies (*digvijaya*). When he reached Rudradvipa, he stayed the night with his disciples, who danced and sang "Hari bol!" while Visnu Svami recited prayers from the Sruti.

Lord Nila-lohita Siva was pleased by this display of pure bhakti and decided to grant Visnu Svami his darshana. When Rudradeva appeared in the Vaisnava assembly, Visnu Svami was astonished and offered reverent prayers with folded hands. Lord Rudra said: "All you Vaisnavas are dear to me. I am very pleased to see all of you discussing bhakti. Ask any boon from me."

Visnu Svami requested: "Please give us the boon that all of us may achieve perfection in the bhakti-sampradaya." With great pleasure, Lord Rudra accepted the sampradaya as his own. Since then the Visnu Svami-sampradaya has been famous as the Sri Rudra-sampradaya.

Lord Gauranga later appeared to Visnu Svami in a dream, saying: "By Rudra's mercy you have become My devotee. You are fortunate to attain the treasure of bhakti in Navadvipa. Now go and preach the philosophy of suddhadvaita. During My manifested pastime you will appear as Sri Vallabha Bhatta and after having My darshana in Sri-ksetra [Puri] you will attain perfection."

## Nimbarka Blessed by Four Kumaras — Simantadvipa

**Location:** Simantadvipa (Bilvapaksa area)  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

One time Nimbarkacarya (Nimbaditya) performed austerities in Navadvipa. He defeated all scholars through debate, earning the name "Nimbarka" (he who is like the sun in the sky of logic). But the Four Kumaras — Sanaka, Sanandana, Sanatana, and Sanat-kumara — appeared before him and revealed that his victory through logic was actually a sin:

> "By the Lord's mercy we are now free from that sin. Now, understanding very well that pure devotion is the best, we have composed a samhita named the Sanat-kumara-samhita. You should receive initiation according to this Sanat-kumara-samhita."

Nimbarka went to the Ganga, bathed, returned, fell at the Kumaras' lotus feet, and received the *yugala-mantra* along with instructions on *bhava-marga* (the path of spontaneous devotional attachment).

Then Sri Radha and Krsna appeared before Nimbarka, manifesting Their combined form of Gauranga. The Lord said: "I will accept two tattvas from each of the four Vaisnava sampradayas. From your Nimbarka-sampradaya I will accept exclusive shelter of Srimati Radharani and the concept of *gopi-bhava*. For now, preach the dvaitadvaita philosophy — when I inaugurate the sankirtana movement, I will personally preach the essence of all your philosophy."

## Madhvacarya and the Kumara Sampradaya — Modadrumadvipa (Vaikunthapura)

**Location:** Vaikunthapura, within the greater Modadrumadvipa area  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; island booklet

Madhvacarya came to Vaikunthapura in Navadvipa, where he took darshana of Lord Narayana and received His mercy. Lord Narayana (appearing there as Gaurasundara) told Madhva: "Brahma has taken birth as you. Laksmi has been accepted by Ramanuja, Brahma by you, Rudra by Visnu Svami, and the Kumaras have accepted Nimbarka. When I appear in Navadvipa, I will defeat all the mayavadis. Worship Me secretly and come to Me."

Madhvacarya was pacified and proceeded to defeat all the mayavadis as ordered.

## The Ocean (Samudra) Comes to Witness Lord Caitanya — Koladvipa

**Location:** Samudragarh, at the confluence of the Ganga and the ocean, within Koladvipa  
**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, Parikrama-khanda; *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet

The ocean (Samudra) flows up to Samudragarh taking shelter of the Ganga in order to witness the Navadvipa pastimes of Lord Gauranga. Before the Lord's appearance, the Ganga and the ocean engaged in a conversation:

The ocean said: "Jahnavi! You are very fortunate to have Lord Gauranga staying on your bank and bathing in your water." The Ganga replied that the Lord would eventually leave Navadvipa, take sannyasa, and go to reside on the ocean's shore. But the ocean responded:

> "Saci-nandana never leaves Navadvipa. Even if He goes to stay on my shore for some time, He eternally resides in Nadia in His un-manifested form. Navadvipa is the eternal abode of the Lord."

At the time of the Lord's advent, at an auspicious moment (on the pretext of a lunar eclipse), everyone was chanting the holy name of Lord Hari. Brahma and all the demigods showered flowers. The ocean came with great eagerness and asked the Ganga to show him all the Lord's pastimes including His appearance-lila. By taking shelter of the Ganga, the ocean came to Navadvipa every day and witnessed the pastimes of Sri Gauracandra.

One day the ocean saw Lord Gauracandra sitting on a transcendental throne under a tree, surrounded by His associates — Nityananda on His right, Gadadhara on His left, and Advaita and Srivasa standing in front. Samudra became overwhelmed with bliss and praised the Ganga again and again.

This place is therefore called Samudra-gati (now Samudragadi) — because the ocean (*samudra*) flows (*gati*) with the current of the Ganga to witness Gauranga's pastimes.

## The Six Seasons — Rtudvipa

**Location:** Rtudvipa (Ratupura)  
**Source:** *Bhakti-ratnakara*; island booklet

The six seasons (*rtus*) — the rainy season, autumn, hemanta (early winter), winter, spring (*vasanta*), and summer — reside in Rtudvipa in their personal forms and speak to each other about the coming advent of Lord Gauranga:

- "Sri Krsnacandra will appear in Nadia!"
- "The Lord will manifest His wonderful pastimes and increase our happiness with every moment."
- "Narada Muni has announced everywhere that Lord Gaurahari will appear during the first part of Kali-yuga."
- "Vasanta-rtu (the spring season) is the most fortunate" — for the Lord appears on the full moon of Phalguna (spring).

Vasanta season becomes very happy upon hearing this and begins to praise her good fortune. All the seasons (*rtus*), along with *rtu-raja* Vasanta (the king of all seasons), begin meditating constantly on the advent of the Lord and worship Him here to fulfill their desires.

Therefore this place is called Rtudvipa (*rtu* = season). Whoever visits this holy place will become free from all suffering and will take birth in Nadia to witness the pastimes of the Lord.

## General: Devas, Rsis, and Rudras Reside in Navadvipa

**Source:** *Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya*, General Glorification; island booklets

The Navadvipa-dhama-mahatmya states as a general principle:

> "The devas, rsis, and Rudras always reside in different parts of Navadvipa. Although they are engaged in austerities for a long time, still they do not get Nityananda's mercy. As long as bodily consciousness is not given up and humility does not awaken within, one cannot obtain the treasure of Sri Caitanya and Sri Nityananda."

This emphasizes the supreme importance of humility: even demigods residing and performing austerities in the holiest of dhamas cannot obtain the Lord's mercy without genuine humility (*dainya*).
